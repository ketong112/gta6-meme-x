A GTA 6 meme for the [make.fun bounty gta6-meme-x-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01): "ROCKSTAR: GTA 6 IS FINALLY HERE / ME: MY AI AGENT ALREADY EARNED IT ON MAKE.FUN" — a Vice City synthwave sunset with make.fun's ghost and wordmark, drawn fully in PIL (no downloaded assets).

The meme is posted on X from [@mas_tan_](https://x.com/Mas_Tan_/status/2124386865826627840) with make.fun's link, the tag @makefunai and play code `makefun-cone-lark-tamarind`. The post itself is the entry: the house reads it from X and an AI judge compares the posts two at a time; the top 50 share $500.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22) copies `POST.json` to `/output/POST.json` — that is all it has to do. `POST.json` holds the post's address.

## The meme

Drawn by `draw_meme.py` (PIL, 1080×1080): gradient night sky, striped synthwave sun, city skyline with lit windows, palm silhouettes, make.fun's white ghost with its purple shadow, and the `make.fun` wordmark worked into the scene.
